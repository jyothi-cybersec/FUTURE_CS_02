INITIAL TECHNICAL ASSESSMENT

The raw headers show failures for SPF, DKIM, and DMARC. The sending
infrastructure is also associated with the hostname
vps-291847.contabo.net rather than an infrastructure that is clearly
identified as Microsoft's.

These observations provide technical evidence supporting further
phishing investigation.

No claim is made about the ownership of the IP address beyond what is
shown directly in the raw email headers.
