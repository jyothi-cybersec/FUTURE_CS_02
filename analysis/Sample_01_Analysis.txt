SAMPLE 01 — PHISHING EMAIL ANALYSIS

Risk Classification: PHISHING

EVIDENCE-BASED INDICATORS

1. Unidentified sender
The sender is presented only as "Security Team" without a specific
organization or identifiable sender address.

2. Generic greeting
The message begins with "Dear User" rather than identifying the
recipient by name.

3. Urgency
The email creates pressure by stating that the account will be
permanently locked within 24 hours.

4. Fear-based language
The message claims that suspicious activity has been detected and
threatens account suspension.

5. Suspicious verification request
The recipient is instructed to verify account details through a link.

6. Suspicious URL
The message contains:
http://secure-account-verify[.]com

The domain does not identify a known legitimate organization.

7. Sensitive-action request
The requested account verification could lead a recipient to disclose
credentials or other sensitive information.

OVERALL ASSESSMENT

The combination of an unidentified sender, generic greeting, urgency,
fear-based account-lock warning, suspicious domain, and account
verification request indicates a phishing attempt.

HEADER LIMITATION

Raw email headers were not provided with the Future Interns sample.
Therefore, SPF, DKIM, DMARC, Return-Path, Received headers, and sending
infrastructure cannot be verified.

RECOMMENDED ACTION

Do not click the link or provide credentials, OTPs, passwords, or
personal information. Report the message as phishing and verify any
account issue through the organization's official website or a known
trusted support channel.
