SAMPLE 02 — LINK ANALYSIS

Observed URL:
https://bit.ly/3vF9xKz

URL Handling:
The URL was extracted from the raw email source. It was not opened or
visited.

Analysis:

1. The link uses the bit.ly URL-shortening service.

2. The shortened URL does not reveal the final destination to the
recipient from the visible link alone.

3. The visible link therefore does not establish that the destination
belongs to Microsoft.

4. The email uses Microsoft branding and asks the recipient to review
recent account activity, making the destination especially important to
verify before clicking.

Assessment:
The shortened URL is a phishing indicator that requires caution and
further safe, passive investigation.

No claim is made about the final redirect destination because the URL
was not opened.
